Resource reader cleanup and consolidation
2026-10-20

The selected reader preserves all 221 previously correct fields. The cleanup
does not recover any of the 18 remaining readable misses.

Removed
- HP/Mana bar/caption fusion and temporal confirmation: no original-reading
  recovery in the full benchmark; the combined layer increased reading time.
- Native whole-number font fitting: no gain and one regression.
- Native-font calibration: the selected robust backend did not consume it.
- Their dedicated factories, tests and three experiment workflows.
- Fifteen verbose historical JSON reports from the active tree. The archive
  index retains their hashes and immutable Git links; historical TXT reports stay.

Retained
- Current-frame panel and individual-row structure, identity and visibility checks.
- The existing robust numeric threshold consensus and bounded glyph caches.
- A 32-entry geometry cache keyed by complete current evidence pixels.
- Duplicate-panel rejection and UNKNOWN/N/A for unsupported or ambiguous readings.
- One opt-in entry point: tools.resource_reader.CurrentResourceReader.
  Production defaults remain unchanged.

The geometry cache was tested separately from unused font calibration. Two
development originals, with opposite ON/OFF ordering, preserved all eight
readings and showed lower cached p95 in all six case/mode comparisons. These
local blockwise measurements were under concurrent load; they justify retaining
the small cache but do not establish live latency or exclude workload drift.
The compact JSON records sample counts, medians, p95, order and raw-file hash.

Timings cover reading after discovery; capture, copying and discovery are
excluded. This is offline algorithm timing, not a live freshness measurement or
a hard deadline. The previously inspected 80/20 split is regression evidence.

The initial hosted run passed all 12 shards and 114 guards, but its final
summary rejected three existing parameterized test names containing "::".
The parser now preserves complete parameter identities. A new regression also
rejects a substituted parameter with matching counts and refreshed XML hashes.
The complete benchmark and 115 guards were rerun after this reporting-only fix.
